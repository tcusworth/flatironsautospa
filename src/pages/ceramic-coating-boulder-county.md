## Why Boulder County drivers choose ceramic coating

Colorado is demanding on paint: intense sun at altitude, snow and winter road treatment, spring pollen, and dust from mountain and trailhead drives. A ceramic coating adds a durable, slick layer that helps repel that everyday contamination and makes washing easier. Many owners choose a coating on a new vehicle, after [paint correction](/paint-correction/), or on a car they plan to keep for years.

## What to know before you book

- A coating adds gloss and protection but doesn't make paint scratch-proof or remove the need to wash the vehicle.
- Paint prep and any needed correction happen first, because the coating seals in whatever is underneath it.
- How long a coating lasts depends on prep quality, coating type, storage, washing habits, and weather exposure.

## Detailing for how Boulder drives

Boulder drivers get a mix of conditions that are hard on a vehicle: strong high-altitude sun, snow and road treatment in winter, spring pollen, and dust from trailheads and canyon roads. Salt and road film settle on paint, wheels, and lower panels, while dust and pet hair from outdoor-minded households end up in the cabin. Regular detailing keeps paint protected and the interior comfortable.

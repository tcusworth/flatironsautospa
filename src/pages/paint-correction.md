## Paint correction for a cleaner finish.

Paint correction is for vehicles with swirl marks, wash haze, oxidation, or light surface defects that make the paint look dull under sunlight.

The process is tailored to the vehicle condition and finish goals. It can improve clarity before sealant or ceramic coating and is especially valuable for dark paint.

- Best for black, dark blue, red, luxury, and enthusiast vehicles.
- Deep scratches may need touch-up or body repair.
- Pairs naturally with ceramic coating.

## Paint correction for a cleaner finish.

Paint correction is for vehicles with swirl marks, wash haze, oxidation, or light surface defects that make the paint look dull under sunlight.

The process is tailored to the vehicle condition and finish goals. It can improve clarity before sealant or ceramic coating and is especially valuable for dark paint.

- Best for black, dark blue, red, luxury, and enthusiast vehicles.
- Deep scratches may need touch-up or body repair.
- Pairs naturally with [ceramic coating](/ceramic-coating/).

## What swirls and haze are, and how polishing helps

Swirl marks are fine scratches in the clear coat, the transparent top layer of the paint. They usually come from washing and drying, and they show up as circular marks or a dull haze under direct sun or bright light. Dark and bold colors show them the most.

Polishing carefully levels the surface of the clear coat so those fine defects are reduced and the paint reflects light more cleanly. Because clear coat is a finite layer, the goal is to improve the finish while leaving as much of it in place as possible.

## What paint correction can and can't fix

- **Usually improved:** swirl marks, wash haze, light scratches, and oxidation on the surface.
- **Often beyond detailing:** deep scratches, rock chips, and damage that goes through the clear coat. These may need touch-up or paint repair.

A short evaluation and conversation before the work helps set honest expectations for your paint. The free consultation is also where the right level of correction or protection is discussed.

## Protect the result

Polished paint looks its best when it's protected. A sealant or [ceramic coating](/ceramic-coating/) helps keep the finish clean and glossy, and regular [maintenance washes](/maintenance-wash/) help avoid putting new swirl marks back in.

## Interior auto detailing in Longmont and Boulder.

Flatirons Auto Spa interior detailing focuses on the areas you touch every day: seats, carpets, mats, panels, console areas, glass, vents, and door jambs. The process is designed to restore comfort without over-wetting or over-treating delicate materials.

Choose this service when your cabin has daily dust, winter grime, pet hair, food residue, light odor, or a general tired feel.

- Best for daily drivers, family SUVs, luxury cabins, and pre-sale refreshes.
- Pair with [exterior detailing](/exterior-detailing/) for a complete inside-and-out result.
- Ask about extra time for heavy pet hair or odor issues.

## What interior detailing covers

A quick vacuum tidies a cabin. A detail goes after the places a quick clean skips: seams and crevices where grit collects, carpet fibers and floor mats, door panels and vents, the console, and the glass. Steam cleaning uses heat to loosen built-up grime from fabrics and hard surfaces, so surfaces get clean without being soaked, and leather and vinyl are cared for as materials rather than simply wiped down.

Leather and dashboards need regular attention in Colorado. Strong sun at altitude and dry air can dry out and fade interior surfaces over time, which is why the Prestige Protection package adds leather care and UV interior protection.

## Pet hair, odors, and winter grime

Pet hair weaves into carpet and cargo-area fibers, so heavy hair takes extra time to remove. Mention it when you book so the appointment has enough room. Odors from food, pets, smoke, or moisture are best handled by cleaning their source rather than covering them up, and steam cleaning helps reduce many of them. Severe odors can take more than one treatment depending on where they come from.

Winter adds its own mess: salt, slush, and sand tracked onto carpets and mats leave stains and a gritty feel. An interior detail after winter is a good reset for the whole cabin.

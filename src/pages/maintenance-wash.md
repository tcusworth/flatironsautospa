## Maintenance wash service for Boulder County.

Maintenance washing is ideal after a premium detail or ceramic coating. It removes dust, pollen, road film, and light grime before buildup gets harder to clean.

It is a smart option for drivers who want their vehicle to stay consistently presentable without booking a full detail every time.

- Useful after snow, rain, pollen, or dusty mountain drives.
- Supports [ceramic coating](/ceramic-coating/) and sealant performance.
- Best when scheduled regularly.

## Why regular washing matters

Dirt, pollen, and road film left on paint are abrasive, and winter salt and slush can sit on wheels, jambs, and lower panels. Washing them off regularly, with gentle methods, keeps the finish looking good and keeps a sealant or coating working as intended. Waiting until the buildup is heavy makes the vehicle harder to clean and raises the chance of marring the paint.

## Maintenance wash vs. full detail

- **Maintenance wash:** keeps a recently detailed or coated vehicle clean between appointments.
- **Full detail:** a deeper reset of the interior and exterior, with polishing and protection depending on the package. See [interior detailing](/interior-detailing/) and [exterior detailing](/exterior-detailing/).

Many drivers use both: a full detail to start fresh, then regular maintenance washes to keep it that way. Call to talk through a schedule that suits how and where you drive.

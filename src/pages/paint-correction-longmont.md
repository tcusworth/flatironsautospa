Flatirons Auto Spa helps Longmont drivers improve paint clarity through careful polishing. This is especially useful for black paint, red paint, luxury vehicles, classic vehicles, and cars that show wash marks under direct light.

## Is your paint a candidate?

Swirl marks, wash haze, and light oxidation are the most common reasons drivers book paint correction. Park in direct sun or look under bright light: if the paint looks dull, hazy, or covered in fine circular marks, polishing can often improve it. Dark and bold colors tend to show these defects the most.

Deep scratches, rock chips, and damage that goes through the clear coat are a different story. Polishing can't restore missing paint, so those may need touch-up or paint repair.

## How correction works

Polishing carefully levels the surface of the clear coat so fine defects are reduced and the paint reflects light more cleanly. Because clear coat is a finite layer, the aim is the best finish for your paint while keeping as much of it as possible. The evaluation and consultation before the work set realistic expectations.

## Protect the finish

Once the paint looks its best, protecting it keeps it that way. A premium sealant or [ceramic coating](/ceramic-coating/) helps resist contamination and makes washing easier, and regular [maintenance washes](/maintenance-wash/) help avoid introducing new swirls.

## Exterior auto detailing in Longmont and Boulder.

Exterior detailing cleans and protects the most visible parts of your vehicle: paint, wheels, trim, glass, tires, and high-contact exterior surfaces. It is ideal after road trips, winter salt, pollen season, or when the finish needs more clarity.

Flatirons Auto Spa uses careful wash methods and finishing products chosen to improve gloss while respecting the condition of the paint.

- Great for restoring shine before events, resale, or seasonal protection.
- Pairs well with [paint correction](/paint-correction/) for swirl reduction.
- Can be upgraded with [ceramic protection](/ceramic-coating/).

## Hand wash, wheels, and finishing

A hand wash lets a detailer reach the places automatic washes tend to miss: wheel faces, tires, door jambs, and the lower panels where road film settles. Wash technique matters for the paint itself too, since brush-style automatic washes are a common source of fine swirl marks, especially on dark finishes.

After the wash, the finish is protected so water beads and dirt releases more easily next time. The package you choose sets how far that goes.

## Wax, sealant, or ceramic?

- **Wax sealant** (Essential Elegance) is a good everyday layer of gloss and protection.
- **Polish and premium sealant** (Prestige Protection) refines the finish first, then adds longer-lasting protection.
- **Ceramic coating** (Grand Reserve Executive Spa) is the most durable option and the easiest to keep clean. See [ceramic coating](/ceramic-coating/) for how it works.

## Built for Colorado driving

Winter salt, slush, and road film sit on paint, wheels, jambs, and lower panels, and strong high-altitude sun is hard on unprotected paint. Pollen season and dusty trail drives add their own buildup. A wash and protection service after winter clears that buildup and prepares the vehicle for spring, and protection before winter makes it easier to wash off afterward.
